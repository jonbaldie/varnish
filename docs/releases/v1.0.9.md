# v1.0.9

## Fixes

- Invalidate cached entries after every successful unsafe method, not only POST, PUT, PATCH, and DELETE.
- Encode spaces and horizontal tabs in `Location` and `Content-Location` references so their invalidation bans are valid.
- Honor positive `Surrogate-Control` `max-age` and its freshness extension as the TTL and grace, overriding `Cache-Control` and `Expires`.
- Treat zero `Surrogate-Control` `max-age`, with or without a freshness extension, as uncacheable.
- Treat `Expires` dates at the Unix Epoch as already expired, and recognize `s-maxage` as a `Cache-Control` freshness override.

## Tests

- Share one Compose fixture lifecycle across test suites.
- Remove a startup race from the fixture readiness-timeout test.

## Verification

- `COMPOSE_PARALLEL_LIMIT=1 make -j1 test test-e2e-hard`
